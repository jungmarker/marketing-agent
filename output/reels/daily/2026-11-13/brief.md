## Gear and settings

- Rear camera, 1x lens, vertical, 4K or 1080p at 30 fps. **HDR video off** (the
  editor does not tone-map it). Lock focus and exposure on your face.
- Tripod at eye level. Clip-on or wired mic; check one clip with headphones
  before you shoot the rest.
- Shade or overcast beats direct sun. Sun behind the phone, never behind you.
- Leave the top fifth of the frame empty above your head on b01, b02, b04,
  b08 and b11. That is where the text card goes.
- Solid top, no small patterns. Nothing with another brokerage's sign in frame.

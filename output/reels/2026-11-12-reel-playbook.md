# Reel playbook: three to shoot, and how to shoot them

Built from `.claude/instagram/swipe.md` (1,084 Shorts, 29 accounts, ranked by
multiple over each account's own median) plus published breakdowns of real
estate reels. Read the caveats in the swipe file first: the view data is
YouTube Shorts, not Instagram, and "hook" there means title.

Every number in `{{braces}}` is a placeholder. The neighborhood medians come
from `config.py`, which is not a live source. Pull each one from Bright MLS
before recording, and have your title company confirm the ground rent lines.
Nothing here has been checked against your brokerage's advertising rules.

---

## The setup (same for all three)

**Evidence grade:** A = from the ranked data. B = platform statement by
Instagram. C = practitioner write-ups with self-reported numbers.

| Thing | Do this | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | Phone vertical, 1080x1920, rear camera, 30 fps. Eye level, chest-up, with a clear band of space above your head for the text block | C |
| Safe zone | All text between y=230 and y=1440, and off the right 230 px. Instagram's interface covers the rest | skill rule |
| Hook text | One block above your head, 6 words or fewer, on screen at frame 1. It names the topic before anyone hears you | C |
| Three hooks | Spoken line, on-screen line and first caption line are written separately and say different things | C |
| First frame | Already moving: walking into frame, pointing at the thing, door opening. Not a still face | A (inspector reels) |
| Cuts | New shot every line, roughly every 2.5 to 4 s. Room reveals stay up at least 2 s | C |
| Length | 25 to 35 s for these scripts. A 5 to 10 s text-over-b-roll version is the low-effort alternative | C |
| Captions | Burned in for the whole body. Most top accounts skip this, so it is the cheap edge | C |
| Light and sound | Face a window or shoot outside in shade. Wired or clip-on mic. Bad audio costs more than bad video | C |
| Ask | One. A comment keyword that sends something, or a question they can answer in one word | C |
| What ranks | Watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers, so make something a buyer forwards to a partner | B |
| Fair housing | Describe the place and the facts. Never who a neighborhood is "for" | rule |

Caption for each still gets the compliance footer from `config.py`.

## Reel 1: Nobody tells you (ground rent)

**Why this one:** the top two rows in the sample were "what nobody tells you
about [place]". A Maryland agent's ground-rent warning also showed up in the
local set. It is specific to Baltimore rowhouses, which no out-of-town account
can copy.

Formula #3. Hook scored 84 STRONG. 34.5 s, 12 beats, loops on "ground rent".

## Reel 2: Head to head (Hampden or Canton)

**Why this one:** "A or B?" titles hit 3x in 33% of cases against a 14% base
rate, the strongest repeatable pattern found. The closest match, "Windermere or
Winter Park? Which one do you prefer?", did 24x for a 69K-subscriber agent.

**Why this one:** leading-number lists hit 3x at 24%, and the best local
example, "4 Philly suburbs with homes under $250K", did 20x. A price alone in

## What I would not make

- **"Stop doing X" advice reels.** 2 of 39 beat 3x. It is the default agent
  opener and it was the weakest shape in the sample.
- **Famous-house or national-economy content.** Big multiples, wrong audience.
- **Luxury listing films.** The 10M+ examples in the write-ups are produced
  outliers with no lead numbers attached.

## Honest status of these scripts

- Humanizer scores are 50 to 65 (REVIEW, not PASS). `voice.md` is empty, so
  these are in a generic plain voice, not yours. Send three of your own reels
  or captions and they get rewritten the way you talk.
- Nothing was logged to `log.md` and nothing was posted.
